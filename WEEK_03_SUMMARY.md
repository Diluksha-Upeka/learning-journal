### Things to remember
- **Feature Scaling**: Standardization (mean=0, std=1) vs Normalization (0-1 range)
- **Pipelines**: Automate preprocessing + modeling steps
- **Joblib**: Efficiently save/load ML models
    - `joblib.dump(model, 'model.pkl')` to save
    - `model = joblib.load('model.pkl')` to load
